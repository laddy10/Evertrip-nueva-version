# Privacy and personal data processing policy

**EverTrip Viajes**  
**Effective date:** October 22, 2026

## 1. Our commitment

EverTrip Viajes respects its customers’ privacy and processes the personal data it receives through inquiries, quotations and bookings for private transportation and tourism experiences in accordance with Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1074 of 2015 and other applicable Colombian legislation.

This policy explains what data EverTrip receives, the purposes for which it uses that data, how it shares it with drivers and partner operators, and how data subjects can exercise their rights.

## 3. Personal data we may receive

Our main contact channel is WhatsApp. Depending on the customer’s request, we may receive their name, telephone or WhatsApp number, service date and time, pickup location, destination, number of passengers, requested activity and any notes necessary to arrange the booking.

EverTrip does not ordinarily request identity documents, medical information or bank card details during its standard booking process. If other data is exceptionally required, we will explain the purpose and apply the relevant legal requirements.

## 4. Purposes of processing

EverTrip processes data to respond to requests, prepare quotations, coordinate and confirm bookings, contact passengers, assign drivers and operators, provide travel updates, offer follow-up assistance, handle requests or complaints and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Using contact details for advertising or promotions unrelated to the original request will require the appropriate consent where required. Simply sending a WhatsApp message does not constitute unlimited consent for advertising purposes.

## 5. Sharing data with drivers and partner operators

EverTrip coordinates transfers with drivers from its own operation and, when necessary, with external providers. The vehicles in its operation are affiliated with Transportes TQ del Caribe, according to the operational information supplied by EverTrip.

To provide the service, EverTrip may share the passenger’s name, telephone or WhatsApp number, pickup point, destination, date, time and other strictly necessary details with the assigned driver. The driver may contact the passenger directly to coordinate pickup.

For tours and other experiences, EverTrip may share necessary information with partner tourism operators responsible for delivering the activity. Such data will be shared and processed in accordance with the stated purposes, consent and other applicable legal bases. EverTrip will seek to limit access and require the appropriate safeguards from its partners.

## 6. Data requested directly by third parties

During an activity, a tourism operator may directly request additional information for registration, safety or insurance purposes. Where that operator independently determines why and how it processes that information, it is subject to the legal obligations of a data controller for that data.

EverTrip does not ordinarily collect or retain that additional information, without prejudice to its legal obligations concerning information it does receive, use or share.

## 7. WhatsApp and digital services

WhatsApp is the main channel for inquiries and bookings. Use of that platform is also governed by its provider’s terms. EverTrip uses conversations for the purposes described in this policy.

The website may generate technical information essential to its operation. Any additional use of analytics, advertising tools or non-essential cookies must be assessed, disclosed and configured in accordance with applicable legal requirements; this policy does not state that such tools are active without technical verification.

## 8. Consent

Before collecting data, or at the time required by law, EverTrip will explain the purposes of processing and make this policy available. Where the law requires consent, EverTrip will request prior, express and informed consent and retain evidence of it by appropriate means.

If a customer decides not to provide information essential to coordinating a transfer or tour, EverTrip will explain any resulting limitations on the booking.

## 9. Rights of data subjects

Data subjects may access, update and correct their data; request information about its use; request proof of consent where applicable; submit inquiries or complaints; request deletion or withdraw consent in the circumstances permitted by law; access their information free of charge as applicable; and refer matters to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce when the legal requirements are met.

Deletion or withdrawal of consent may be restricted where there is a valid legal or contractual duty to retain certain information.

## 10. Procedure for inquiries and complaints

The person identified as responsible in section 2 will handle data subjects’ requests via **WhatsApp (+57 314 765 9756)** or **email (evertripviajesytours@gmail.com)**. We recommend providing a name, reply channel, description of the request and sufficient details to identify the relevant information. Reasonable identity checks may be carried out to protect the data.

**Inquiries** will be answered within a maximum of ten (10) business days of receipt; if a legally permitted extension is required, the data subject will be informed, and the extension will not exceed five (5) additional business days.

Complete **complaints** will be addressed within fifteen (15) business days counted from the day after receipt; where a legal extension applies, it will not exceed eight (8) additional business days. Incomplete complaints and other procedural steps will be handled in accordance with Law 1581 of 2012.

## 11. Security and retention

EverTrip will adopt reasonable administrative, personnel and technical measures to prevent the loss, misuse, unauthorized access or disclosure of personal data. Information will be made available only to those who need it to deliver the service, within the relevant legal obligations.

Data will be retained for the period necessary for the stated purposes and to meet legal or contractual obligations or address claims. Once those purposes have been fulfilled and no retention obligation remains, EverTrip will delete the data using appropriate procedures.

**Database retention period:** for as long as legitimate processing purposes and applicable retention obligations remain.

In the event of a security incident, EverTrip will assess its scope and take the containment, response and reporting measures required by current legislation.

## 12. Minors and sensitive data

EverTrip avoids requesting unnecessary personal data about minors or sensitive data. If such data must exceptionally be processed, EverTrip will comply with the special requirements of Colombian legislation, including enhanced protection for children and adolescents and the relevant consents.

## 13. Amendments and validity

This policy may be amended in response to regulatory, technological or operational changes. Substantial changes will be communicated as required by law. The current version will be available on the official website.
